# Rule: Prioritize Direct Answers on Diagnostic Inquiries

Whenever a user asks an informational or diagnostic question (e.g., "why is this failing?", "why am I stuck?", "what happened?"):
1. **Answer Upfront**: Provide the direct, concrete explanation and root cause in plain English within the immediate response.
2. **No Silent Digression**: Do not launch background tasks, subagents, or lengthy multi-step verifications without first answering the user's specific question.
3. **Action Following Explanation**: Once the answer is stated, clearly describe what follow-up actions or fixes are being taken or verified.
